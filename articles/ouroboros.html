Do you know what an ouroboros is?

A snake eating its own tail sounds like the end of a game. In an ancient symbol, it can mean a new beginning.

By tmrCEO curiosity desk · October 16, 2026· 2 min read

You might have heard a name like “Oroboro.” The symbol we’re exploring is usually called the ouroboros: a snake, or sometimes a dragon, curving around until its mouth meets its own tail.

A picture with no obvious beginning

Follow the creature around the circle. Where does it start? Where does it finish? That is part of the idea. The University Museum at the University of Tokyo describes the symbol as expressing ideas including infinity and repeated renewal. It is a picture people have used to think about cycles.

One symbol, different stories

Its meaning depends on where you find it. Yale’s exhibition on Egypt describes an old text, Hieroglyphica, that used the tail-eating serpent to represent the universe. A Greek manuscript of that work was rediscovered in 1419, and later translations helped its imagery travel through European art.

So it is more than a strange snake drawing. It is an example of how one simple image can hold a very big question.

The Snake-game connection

In our game, meeting your own tail ends the round. In an ouroboros, that meeting completes the circle. Same shape, very different idea!
