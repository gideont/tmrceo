Welcome to Tomorrow CEO (“tmrCEO,” “we,” or “our”). These terms explain the rules for using tmrceo.com, including our games, stories, bookshelf and project activities. Children bring the ideas and lead the learning; adults help with resources, mentorship and guidance.

The short version

  • Be kind, play fairly and respect other people’s work.
  • Use a gamer nickname and keep private details private.
  • Ask a parent or guardian to help with submissions and participation.
  • Your ideas stay yours. Published contributions get credit.

These terms do not replace any separate parental notice or consent required for a particular feature. Reading this page or using the website does not verify parental consent.

2. Gamer names and high scores

You can play Snake without publishing a name or score. If you choose “Save & join,” your chosen nickname and best score can appear on our public top-ten board. Choose a nickname instead of sharing your full name, school, address, email or other private information.

Guest names and device best scores are remembered in your browser. The shared board keeps one best score per guest browser; a browser is not a verified person or account. You can change your nickname in the game. Clearing browser data can remove your access to that guest record, and we cannot guarantee recovery of lost guest scores.

Play fairly. Do not submit fake scores, automate score farming, impersonate another person, interfere with score checks, or disrupt other visitors. Glitch hunting is welcome; exploiting a glitch to spoil the game for others is not. We may correct or remove invalid scores and inappropriate nicknames. High scores have no cash value, and the board does not promise prizes.

3. A kind place to learn

Do not use the site to bully, threaten, harass or discriminate against anyone. Do not submit hateful, sexually explicit, unlawful or otherwise inappropriate content, spam, malicious links or someone else’s private information. Do not try to access private records or damage the website.

We may review, edit, decline or remove contributions, nicknames or scores, or limit access to a feature, to protect visitors and keep the project useful. Content is not guaranteed to be reviewed immediately. If something concerns you, ask an adult for help and contact us.

4. Discoveries and contributions

Everyone is welcome to suggest an interesting discovery. Share your own words and a source we can check. Only submit material you created or have permission to share. A parent or guardian should help a child with permission where needed.

You keep ownership of your original contribution. By submitting it for possible publication, you give tmrCEO non-exclusive permission, without a fee, to review it, make reasonable edits for accuracy, clarity or suitability, and publish it on this website with your chosen public credit. This permission does not transfer ownership to us.

Submissions do not publish automatically, and sending something does not guarantee publication or payment. Selected posts include an author credit and publication date. If no public name is supplied, we may use an anonymous contributor credit. Contact us about a correction, credit or removal request.
